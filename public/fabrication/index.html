The logistical checklist

Beautiful is the start. Buildable is the point.

Every build starts with a review of how it travels, assembles, survives the venue and gets struck at midnight.

No. 1

Transport

Breaks down to fit a van or box truck. Safe weight for crews. Stairs, elevators and tight doors considered.

No. 2

Assembly

Tool-free or minimal-tool setup, a logical sequence and clear connection points for crews under pressure.

No. 3

Efficiency

Quick room flips, easy late-night breakdown and few loose parts to track.

No. 4

Venue requirements

Stable weight distribution, floor protection and respect for rigging and ceiling limits.

No. 5

Storage

Compact, durable and modular, so the piece earns its place in future designs.

No. 6

Crew workflow

Built for real install teams, with less labor time and predictable setup across events.
