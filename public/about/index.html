About Jersey Strike

It started with late-night breakdowns.

What began as a simple solution for late-night strikes has grown into a full event staffing, logistics and fabrication company built for florists, planners, venues and rental teams.

Today we handle everything from floral processing and studio support to luxury rigging, transportation, setup, room flips and seamless strikes. Our mission is to fill the gaps in your production with a team that feels like your own: reliable, trained, respectful and ready to work.
