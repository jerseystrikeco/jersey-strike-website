What full production covers

Six phases. One crew.

You bring the design. We plan it, build it, move it, install it, flip it and take it all home.

  1. 01 · PlanWalkthrough + production planSite visit, venue rules, load-in and dock access, crew plan, truck plan and a minute-by-minute timeline built from your proposal.
  2. 02 · BuildFabrication + infrastructureCustom mechanics, ceremony structures, ceiling frameworks and rentals from our library, engineered to travel, assemble fast and strike at midnight.
  3. 03 · MoveTrucking + logisticsVans to 26ft box trucks, drivers, load plans and city routes. Studio pickup, venue delivery and the return trip.
  4. 04 · InstallRigging, ceilings + large installsChandelier and lighting rigging, suspended florals, tent installs, ceremony structures, tablescapes and detail placement.
  5. 05 · FlipRoom flips + day-of supportCeremony to cocktail hour to reception, on the clock and out of guests’ sight, with a crew lead on site all day.
  6. 06 · Strike + close-outStrike, counts + reportPack-out inside the venue’s hard out, inventory counted with photos, rentals returned and a post-event report that closes the job.
