Accessibility

Our commitment

Jersey Strike wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use our website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and we review the site as we update it.

Animations on this site respect your device’s reduced-motion setting, and images include text descriptions. Our inquiry form works with a keyboard and shows one question at a time.

Prefer not to use the form?

You can email your event details and documents to inquire@jerseystrike.com or call (732) 759-4003, Mon–Fri, 9am–3pm, and our team will take it from there.

Need help or found a problem?

If you have trouble using any part of the site, email inquire@jerseystrike.com and tell us the page and the issue. We will respond and provide the information you need in another way.
