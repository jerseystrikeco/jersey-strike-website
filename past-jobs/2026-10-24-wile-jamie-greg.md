## Lesson learned (post-event)
Larry: "We really could have used 4 people" on the Saturday strike at The Reeds. The team of 3 was short for ~300 rental pieces + 21 ceremony pieces in a 10pm–midnight window. The job-sizing method (≈37 work units ÷ 5 per person-hour ÷ 2 hr window) says 4. For similar Wile weddings with this volume, strike with 4.
