## Patterns worth knowing
- **Grandfathered / discounts:** A Lily Among Thorns gets a 25% "family" discount on the full total (after the 8% admin fee). Wile gets $50/hr install, $65 strike and 5% admin.
- **Minimums:** many small jobs went out under $1,500 (UEM $1,018, Sticks + Stones $1,134, Sage $1,134, Elizabeth Hall $1,232, Wile West Creek $1,276, Hey Sunshine $1,458, Mixed Company $1,575 total). Full-service jobs often went out under $5,000.
- **Client truck management** has been billed at $1/mile with no $200 minimum (34, 70, 110 mi).
- **Destination work:** travel time billed hourly per person; lodging passed through ($300–470); meal stipend ~$62.50/person/day; one $300 base for a multi-night trip.
- **JS rentals and fab:** wooden chuppah rental $950; white plinths $75 each; 6×6 steel grid $150 (sales tax applies on rental-only orders); chandelier rings $100 each; custom frames $450–510; c-clamp packages ~$600; site visit $650; linen count/repack fee $300; NYC tolls/parking $50.
- **Ladders:** 8–12 ft $50; 12–24 ft (Skyscraper) $150; rigging kit $100 (sometimes "rigging essentials" $300 for big ceiling jobs).
- **Typical strike crews:** 2 for small/medium, 3 for ~100–300 pieces, 4–6 for large weddings or multi-room; 4–7 hrs portal to portal locally, 8–11 hrs for Cape May/MD/PA studios.
