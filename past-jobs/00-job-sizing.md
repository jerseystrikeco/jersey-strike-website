# How to size a job (team, hours): method + benchmarks

Built from ~490 Jersey Strike quotes in Trellis (2024 → 2027) + the pricing formula. Use it every time.

## Benchmarks from history (portal-to-portal hours)
| | Team size (share of quotes) | Hours: 25th / median / 75th pct | Person-hours median |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strike / breakdown (259 crew calls) | 2 people 47% · 4 people 22% · 3 people 16% · 1 person 9% · 5+ 5% | 4.25 / 5.75 / 7.5 | 14 |
| Install / setup (160 crew calls) | 2 people 54% · 3 people 13% · 4 people 11% · 1 person 14% · 5+ 8% | 6 / 8 / 10.75 | 17 |

Typical shapes:
- Local NJ strike, ≤10 tables or a handful of installs → **2 people, 4–5 hrs**.
- Wedding strike, 15–25 tables + ceremony pieces + rentals to studio → **3–4 people, 5–7 hrs**.
- Big/multi-room strike (30+ tables, ceiling, chuppah, 300+ rental pieces) → **4–6 people, 6–9 hrs**.
- Ceiling/smilax/chandelier installs → 2–4 people, 6–11 hrs, + ladders (12–24 ft $150 each) + rigging kit. Removal of the same ceiling ≈ 40–50% of install time.
- Cape May / Philly / Hudson Valley / CT / Long Island add 2–4 hrs of drive per call; MD/DC/Berkshires/Chicago are destination jobs (travel hours billed, hotel, meals).

## Algorithm (do these steps, show your work in `reasoning`)
1. **List zones** from the docs/scope (ceremony, cocktail, reception tables, bars/sweetheart/cake, ceiling/overhead, entry/exterior, flips, studio unload). Every zone gets 1–2 people.
2. **Count work units** per zone:
   - 1 unit = one guest table setting (CP + candles/votives), one cocktail-table group, one bar, one escort/sweetheart/cake display.
   - 0.5 unit = a bud-vase cluster or small piece (votive sets, signage, 10 loose bud vases).
   - 2 units = arch/chuppah/ceremony meadow cluster, large urn/pedestal pair, mantle/fireplace, staircase, tent entrance.
   - 3 units per chandelier or per ~10 ft of ceiling/beam greenery (needs ladders); 4+ for hung structures/grids.
   - Linens: 1 unit per ~25 linens to count/bag. Furniture/chairs: 1 unit per ~25 chairs moved.
3. **Convert to on-site person-hours.** Strike ≈ **5 units per person-hour** (incl. careful packing + strapping). Install/design ≈ **2 units per person-hour** (slower: placement, fluffing, candles). Ceiling/rigging: use 1–1.5 units/ph for install.
4. **Fit the window.** Team = person-hours ÷ the client's window (load-in → photo ready, or breakdown start → hard stop / departure), rounded up, min 2 for night strikes and anything with ladders, max ~1 per zone pair. If the window forces more than 6 people, flag it.
5. **Build the portal-to-portal timeline** (pricing formula steps) using drive times; add 0.5–1 hr studio unload/organize when returning rentals, +0.25–0.5 hr if resetting the client's truck, +1–1.5 hr to load a 26 ft truck (1.5 for holidays).
6. **Sanity check** against the 2–3 most similar past jobs (same florist, venue or scope size). If your hours or team differ by more than ~30% from those, explain why or adjust.
7. **Flag uncertainty**: no inventory list, "TBD" scope, unknown dock/parking, NYC parking/security, union venues, overhead work, second locations.

## Notes on old prices in the history
- 2024: install often $40/hr, base $200, admin 5%, fuel $0.65–0.70/mi.
- 2025: install $45/hr, strike $65, base $300 by mid-year, admin 8%.
- 2026: $65 for everything (Wile + a few long-time florists still at $50 on some lines). Premium rates ($75–85/hr) appear on some NYC/late/destination quotes.
- Many archived inquiries were declined or never answered; treat them as scope/hours references, not as proof the price was right.
